01 / The Problem

Emergency callers are flying blind.
So are dispatchers.

Shock, panic, darkness, language barriers — when seconds count, critical information never reaches the people who need it. GUARDIAN changes that.

02 / Capabilities

Seven layers of intelligence,
activated in one call.

GUARDIAN is engineered for reliability under the most extreme conditions — zero latency, zero user input, maximum data.

Dual Camera Activation

Front and rear cameras activate simultaneously. The dispatcher chooses the angle. No user action required.

Real-Time GPS Stream

Exact coordinates transmitted live. Response teams know the location before arrival — every second of the way.

Live Video to Dispatch

Dispatchers view the scene in real time with screenshot capability. Footage is relayed instantly to units en route.

Forensic Recording

All video and audio encrypted, timestamped, and stored in tamper-proof cloud. Legally admissible evidence.

AI Voice & Sound ID

Artificial intelligence identifies voices, gunshots, screams, and vehicle sounds for instant perpetrator profiling.

Night Mode + Torch

Automatic low-light enhancement and flashlight activation. Full visibility in complete darkness.

Facial Recognition

AI instantly matches faces against criminal databases. If a match is found, officers know exactly who they're dealing with — before they arrive on scene.

03 / Deployment Strategy

From consumer app
to government infrastructure.

A three-phase rollout designed to generate revenue, prove the model, and scale to institutional partnerships worldwide.

Phase I

B2C Launch

Consumer app. Activates automatically on 911, 112, or 999 call. Sends live link to family emergency contacts. Evidence stored in personal vault. No government integration required. Immediate market entry.

Phase II

B2G Integration

Municipal partnerships with police, fire, rescue, and mountain rescue services. EU Horizon funding eligible. Government SLA contracts. Integration with national dispatch systems.

Phase III

Global Scale

License technology to telecoms and insurers. Strategic acquisition target. NATO and EU institutional partnerships. Mandatory integration with next-generation emergency networks.

04 / Current Status

Phase I is no longer a plan.
It is running.

A working GUARDIAN prototype has been built and tested end to end on iOS. The core evidence chain — capture, locate, secure, notify — functions today, in a bilingual interface, on real hardware.

Automatic Video Capture

Activation triggers the rear camera and records a timestamped 30-second sequence of the scene — no further user input needed.

Precise Location Capture

GPS coordinates are captured at the moment of activation and stored alongside the recording.

Encrypted Evidence Vault

Every recording is uploaded to private, access-controlled cloud storage and retrievable only by the account holder.

Emergency Contact Alert

A stored emergency contact is notified with the incident location immediately after the recording completes.

Available for demonstration. The prototype can be shown live to prospective pilot partners, agencies, and investors. Automated dispatch integration and call-audio capture remain deliberately out of scope pending regulatory and legal review.
